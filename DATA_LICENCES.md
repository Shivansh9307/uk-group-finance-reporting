## Notes

- The data are unaudited management information as published by each body. I use them as
  released and do not correct them; where a figure looks wrong I record it rather than change it.
- This project is not endorsed by HM Treasury, NHS England, the Office for National Statistics or
  any government department.
- Anything synthetic or authored by me (for example mapping tables or allocation rules) is listed
  in the real-versus-synthetic register in the [README](README.md) and is kept out of real totals.
